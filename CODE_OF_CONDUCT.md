# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, PRs, or any project space.

Reports: parthkomalwad99@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately; the maintainer will respond within a week and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants depending on severity.
